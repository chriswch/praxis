# Engineering blog posts

For blog posts and long-form technical articles.

- **Inverted Pyramid** — most important first, detail in descending order. Readers scan and bounce if the lead is weak; search and AI retrieval weight the opening. Same root as the Pyramid Principle: conclusion first.
- **Skeleton** — concrete title (with keywords) → hook (the pain or the result) → TL;DR → problem/context → solution → result/takeaway.
- **Show, don't tell** — reproducible code, real numbers, screenshots instead of adjectives. "8s → 200ms", not "much faster."
- **One post, one idea** — narrow and deep beats broad and shallow.
- **Learning in public (Julia Evans style)** — concrete, small, honest about where you got stuck. "How I figured this out" lands better with engineers than "look how much I know" — and is easier to write.
- **Discoverability (2026)** — a clear H2/H3 hierarchy and a point-first opening serve both human scanners and AI summarizers.
